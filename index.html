About Me

I’m passionate about how technology transforms the way we work and create value. After studying Information Systems — B.Sc. Wirtschaftsinformatik at Wismar University of Applied Sciences, M.Sc. IT Management & Consulting at the University of Hamburg, with a semester abroad at the University of St Andrews — I gained hands-on experience in software development, process optimization, and enterprise architecture.

I then spent four years at Deloitte, most recently as a Senior Consultant, where I helped organizations leverage data, analytics, and AI—shaping strategies, building agile teams, and establishing AI governance. I worked with medium to large organizations across industries such as telecommunications, chemicals, consumer goods, and industrial goods, both in Germany and abroad.

I completed my PhD in 2026, focusing on how individuals can benefit from generative AI and how it reshapes human behavior. From Jan–Mar 2025, I expanded this research during a stay at the University of South Florida in Tampa. Since March 2026, I have been a postdoc at the Human-centered Information Systems Engineering (HISE) group, Department of Informatics, University of Zurich (UZH).
